# Gobble & Gone — The Great Turkey Escape

Giblet the turkey is on the Thanksgiving menu, and Farmer Barnaby has the
oven hot and the carving knife out. Run, jump, dash and hide through three
stages — the Farmyard, the Barnyard and the Kitchen — grab corn, dodge
falling and thrown carving knives, jump the ovens, and escape to the forest
before Barnaby catches you.

- **Chase:** Barnaby runs behind you, throws knives, searches the haystacks
  when you hide, and corners you when you slow down. Watch the FARMER meter.
- **Moves:** Arrow keys to move, Space to jump (twice — Giblet flaps),
  Shift to dash through danger, Down to hide in a haystack.
- **Pickups:** corn (combo streaks), pumpkins, golden feathers (dash refresh),
  hearts. Three lives, three stages, one great escape.
- **Original everything:** hand-drawn canvas art, synthesized WebAudio music
  and sound effects, no libraries, no network — pure static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press Space to play. `M` mutes, `P` pauses.
